# Knee MRI Report Supervision

Research results from a report-supervised, multi-plane knee MRI finding classification study.

## Overview

This repository provides selected research results associated with weak supervision from radiology reports for 12 knee MRI findings.

The study investigates the reliability and limitations of report-derived labels, including label coverage, assertion ambiguity, and differences between development-set evaluation and expert-reference evaluation.

## Research Scope

The reported analyses address:

- Report-derived supervision for 12 knee MRI findings.
- Label coverage and agreement with expert-reviewed findings.
- Model discrimination under weak-label and expert-reference evaluation.
- Practical limitations of using clinical reports as a source of training and evaluation labels.

This repository currently provides research results only.

Source code, assertion-rule contracts, patient reports, report-derived clinical text, and private reference artifacts are not publicly distributed.

Any future code release will be subject to manuscript submission, the relevant journal's policies, intellectual property requirements, and authorization from the research team.

## Interpretation

The published summaries should not be interpreted as independent reproduction of the complete report-labeling workflow or as external clinical validation.
